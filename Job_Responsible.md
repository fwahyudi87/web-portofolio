Core Responsibilities of Head of Information Technology

1. Systems Architecture & Strategy

Retail & Distribution Ecosystem: Design and scale an integrated technology architecture that supports high-volume retail operations and complex distribution networks (ERP, POS, WMS, and CRM).
Future-Proofing: Evaluate and implement emerging technologies, specifically identifying opportunities to integrate AI tools and automation to streamline business operations and improve employee productivity.
Data Flow & Security: Establish secure, efficient data pipelines between point-of-sale systems, warehouses, and the central office, ensuring strict compliance with local data protection regulations.
2. Execution & Project Delivery

Deployment & Integration: Lead the end-to-end rollout of new retail systems, store openings, and distribution center upgrades on time and within budget.
Vendor Management: Oversee relationships with technology partners and software vendors, negotiating contracts and holding them accountable to SLAs.
Performance Monitoring: Establish robust KPIs for system uptime, network reliability, and operational efficiency, acting swiftly to resolve bottlenecks.
3. Customer Service & Operational Support

Store-Level Excellence: Ensure all retail locations have resilient, high-speed, and reliable technology to support a frictionless checkout and exceptional customer experience.
Internal Helpdesk: Cultivate a service-oriented IT culture that provides rapid, empathetic support to internal staff, from the warehouse floor to the executive suite.
Omnichannel Support: Maintain the backend infrastructure required to provide a seamless bridge between online e-commerce platforms and offline brick-and-mortar stores.
4. Group Synergy

Strategic Alignment: Work closely with the IT leadership of Kanmo Group to align technology stacks, share best practices, and avoid redundant software investments.
Resource Collaboration: Co-manage shared IT infrastructure and cross-functional task forces for group-wide security audits, software migrations, and platform integrations.


Qualifications

Experience: 10+ years of progressive IT experience, with at least 5 years in a senior leadership role within the retail, distribution, or FMCG sectors.
Technical Expertise: Deep knowledge of modern retail architectures, warehouse management systems, and enterprise resource planning software.
Leadership: Proven track record of managing cross-functional teams and successfully delivering complex digital transformation projects.
Collaboration: Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to navigate matrixed organizations and collaborate effectively with sibling companies.
