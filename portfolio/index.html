About

"I lead technology as a force multiplier — pairing deep infrastructure knowledge with cross-functional teams to ship secure, compliant, and resilient retail systems."

As Head of IT at PT. Multidaya Dinamika, I've built hands-on IT experience since 2014 and have led engineering teams since 2019. I own the company's end-to-end technology stack — from the Odoo ERP backbone to POS, fintech, and distribution systems across all our products.
